Hi, I'm Luis David.

I am an Associate Professor at the University of Southern Denmark working on structural dynamics, system identification, and structural health monitoring. My research combines physical modeling, statistical inference, signal processing, and machine learning to understand complex engineering systems from real-world data.

From offshore wind turbines and maritime systems to industrial machinery and civil structures, I develop methods that help monitor, diagnose, and predict system behavior under changing environmental and operational conditions.

About

Welcome! I am Luis David Avendaño-Valencia, an Associate Professor passionate about understanding how complex engineering systems behave, adapt, and evolve over time. My work combines structural dynamics, data-driven modeling, signal processing, and machine learning to extract meaningful insights from real-world observations. Here you can explore my research, publications, teaching, and the projects I am fortunate to develop with students and collaborators around the world.

Research Interests

  • Structural Dynamics
  • Structural Health Monitoring
  • System Identification
  • Non-Stationary Dynamic Systems
  • Statistical Signal Processing
  • Machine Learning for Engineering Systems
  • Digital Twins
  • Uncertainty Quantification

Research Themes

Structural Health Monitoring

Monitoring and diagnostics of civil and mechanical structures using operational vibration measurements and long-term monitoring data.

Offshore Wind Turbines

Studying environmental and operational effects on structural dynamics to improve reliability and asset management.

System Identification

Development of physics-informed and data-driven methods for dynamic systems with time-varying characteristics.

Digital Twins

Hybrid modeling and predictive monitoring solutions for industrial equipment and engineering infrastructure.
